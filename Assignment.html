When Did Dinosaurs Live?

Dinosaurs lived during the Mesozoic Era, from about 252 million to 66 million years ago. They ruled the Earth for millions of years and came in many different shapes and sizes.

Fun Fact: Modern birds are descendants of certain dinosaurs.
